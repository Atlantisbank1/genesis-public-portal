PHASE 9.4-P8.24
CUSTOMER COMMERCIAL REVIEW SEMANTICS

IMPLEMENTED PRODUCT BOUNDARY

1. Standard Family Membership is the immediately payable
   commercial product in this phase.

2. The customer page presents 14 standard services as
   included services.

3. The customer page presents 5 separately scoped services
   as Custom Quote services.

4. Custom Quote services do not mutate the Standard Family
   Membership PaymentIntent amount.

5. Customer commercial confirmation is required in the UI
   before payment-method submission.

6. Customer selects payment method.

7. Customer does not submit authoritative:
   - amount;
   - currency;
   - invitationId;
   - plan price;
   - settlement status;
   - payment status;
   - membership status.

8. Customer PaymentIntent POST body remains:
   - paymentAccessToken;
   - paymentMethod.

9. Server-authoritative membership pricing remains unchanged.

10. Payment-method selection is not settlement confirmation.

11. Commercial confirmation is not membership activation,
    registration authorization, or entitlement activation.

12. Raw payment-access capability remains transient:
    - query token ingested into React state;
    - visible URL scrubbed;
    - no localStorage;
    - no sessionStorage.

13. No Prisma schema change was required.

14. No migration was required.

15. No PaymentIntent API contract change was required.

16. No settlement implementation was changed.

17. No registration implementation was changed.

18. No membership implementation was changed.

OUT OF SCOPE

- persistent basket model;
- customer-authored pricing;
- automatic pricing of Custom Quote services;
- Custom Quote approval engine;
- settlement confirmation;
- registration-token issuance;
- membership activation.
